Welcome to the digital story card of Chiang Mai Wellness Hill. Explore local products and crafts through playful illustrations, then tap each card to discover the people, place and process behind it.

Why an interactive
story system?

Offline materials make people stop and look. The QR code then opens a second layer of information where visitors can explore the story behind each local product.

01

Offline: First, make people stop.

Hand-drawn graphics, cardboard, stickers and original illustrations make the physical display feel like a handmade Chiang Mai story map rather than a traditional sign.

02

Online: Let people explore.

After scanning the QR code, the webpage becomes a second information layer. Visitors can tap product cards and flip them to discover origin, makers, materials and process.

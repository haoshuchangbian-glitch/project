01 / FRESH AVOCADO

Fresh Avocado

With its smooth, creamy texture and mild natural flavor, avocado is a favorite ingredient in salads, toast, and balanced meals.

Product Overview

Avocados provide unsaturated fats, dietary fiber, and a variety of nutrients. Their creamy flesh can be enjoyed on its own or used in smoothies, salads, and sandwiches.

Serving Tips

An avocado is often ready to eat when it yields slightly to gentle pressure. After cutting, add a little lemon juice to help slow browning.

02 / COFFEE EXPERIENCE

Coffee & Coffee Beans

Product Overview

After roasting and grinding, coffee beans may reveal nutty, chocolatey, or fruity notes. The final flavor depends on the bean variety, origin, roast, and brewing method.
